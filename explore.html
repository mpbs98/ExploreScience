Space

Exploring the vast universe, stars, and galaxies.

Humanity's last challenge, Cosmos itself. Unfathomable sizes, speeds, temperatures, distances...

One of the most famous examples being that the nearest start to us that isn't the Sun is Proxima Centauri, 4.24 light-years away. It would take the fastest military jet engine 965,000 years traveling at constant, maximum speed of 5239 km/h to reach Proxima.
